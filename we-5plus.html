使用顺序

先按题目类型套结构，再用模板句型组织段落。模板只负责文章骨架，具体论点、解释和例子必须对应题目。

三套核心模板
是否同意：让步—反驳型
先承认反方观点的合理性，再反驳，并说明己方观点带来更多好处。
首段 Nowadays, an increasing number of people are concerned about + [主题词/whether + 句子]. Some argue that + 题目改写. I generally agree/disagree with this view. 第二段 Admittedly, proponents of the view that [你反对的观点] raise valid points. In fact, 论点（1—2 句）. For instance, 例子. Thus, it is understandable why some argue that [重申你反对的观点]. 第三段 Nevertheless, these drawbacks are largely outweighed by the benefits that [主题词] brings. To begin with, the positive impact of [主题词] is most evident in [领域]. This is because [解释]. For instance, [具体例证]. Furthermore, [主题词] also plays a crucial role in [另一个方面], as it [进一步说明]. For example, [例子]. Therefore, it is clear that [总结句]. 第四段 In conclusion, while the view [让步观点] may have some merits, I am convinced that [我方观点] is ultimately more beneficial/justifiable, as it not only offers ... but also provides ....
你的观点类（A or B）
题目给出一个现象或两个对立观点，首段必须直接点明你支持哪一方。
让步—反驳型：先写你不支持的观点的合理性，再反驳，并说明你支持的观点的好处更多。 首段 Nowadays, an increasing number of people are concerned about + [主题词/whether + 句子]. Some argue that + 题目中一方观点, while others hold the view that + 对立观点. I generally agree with the former/latter view. 第二段 Admittedly, proponents of [你反对的观点] raise valid points. In fact, 论点（1—2 句）. For example, 例子解释. Thus, it is understandable why some argue that [重申你反对的观点]. 第三段 Nevertheless, these drawbacks are largely outweighed by the benefits that [主题词] brings. To begin with, the positive impact of [主题词] is most evident in [领域]. This is because [解释]. For instance, [具体例证]. Furthermore, [主题词] also plays a crucial role in [另一个方面], as it [进一步说明]. For example, [例子]. Therefore, it is clear that [总结句]. 第四段 In conclusion, while the view [让步观点] may have some merits, I am convinced that [重申我方观点] is ultimately more beneficial/justifiable.
一边倒优缺点类
常见写法是首段明确“优点大于缺点”，第二段让步写一个缺点，第三段集中写优势。
思路 题目给出一个社会现象，询问它是积极还是消极，或要求讨论优缺点并给出自己的看法。首段先明确立场，一般写成“优点大于缺点”。 首段 Nowadays, an increasing number of people are concerned about + [主题词/whether + 句子]. Some argue that + 题目改写. I generally believe that [主题词] yields more advantages than disadvantages to [方面]. 第二段 Admittedly, [主题词] does bring about some negative issues for [方面]. In fact, 论点（1—2 句）. For example, 例子解释. Thus, it is understandable why some argue that the negative impacts of [主题词] cannot be neglected. 第三段 Nevertheless, these drawbacks are largely outweighed by the benefits that [主题词] brings. To begin with, the positive impact of [主题词] is most evident in [领域]. This is because [解释]. For instance, [具体例证]. Furthermore, [主题词] also plays a crucial role in [另一个方面], as it [进一步说明]. For example, [例子]. Therefore, it is clear that [总结句]. 第四段 In conclusion, while [让步观点] may have some merits, I am convinced that [重申我方观点] is ultimately more beneficial/justifiable.
